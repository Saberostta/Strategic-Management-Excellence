Method & thresholds

Recovery = measured ÷ certified × 100. Bias = recovery − 100. Precision is the SD of recovery within the week. Control limits come from the previous 12 weeks of each instrument/element; with fewer than 10 points, all available data is used. Drift is a linear fit of recovery over the last 12 weeks, flagged when |slope| ≥ 0.4 pts/week and R² ≥ 0.2.

Status (defaults shown; per-element limits from the workbook's Limits sheet take precedence): ✕ Red means |bias| > 10%, a 1-3s, 2-2s or R-4s violation, or SD > 6%. ! Amber means |bias| > 5%, a 4-1s or 10-x violation, SD > 3%, drift, or fewer than 90% of results in spec. Otherwise ✓ Green.

Westgard rules
1-3s
single result beyond ±3 SD (random error or gross failure)
2-2s
two consecutive results beyond 2 SD on the same side (systematic shift)
R-4s
consecutive results beyond 2 SD on opposite sides (imprecision)
4-1s
four consecutive results beyond 1 SD on the same side (early bias)
10-x
ten consecutive results on the same side of the mean (persistent bias)
XRF Performance Monitor v1.0.0 · thresholds can be changed in xrf_report/config.py or with command-line flags.
